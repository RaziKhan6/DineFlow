# DineFlow Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap outlines the planned evolution of DineFlow. It serves as a guide for future development and helps prioritize upcoming features.

The roadmap is a living document and may change as the product evolves.

---

# Project Vision

Build a fast, reliable, offline-first restaurant POS that scales from small cafés to large multi-branch restaurants while maintaining a simple and intuitive user experience.

---

# Current Status

Current Phase:

✅ Functional Prototype

Core workflows have been established:

- Table Management
- Order Management
- Kitchen Workflow
- Billing
- Payment

The architecture and design system are stable.

---

# Phase 1 — Foundation

Status: In Progress

Goals:

- Completed Order History
- Order Lifecycle Improvements
- Better Validation
- Performance Optimizations

Success Criteria:

- Orders are preserved after payment.
- Historical sales data becomes available.
- Stable workflow for daily restaurant operations.

---

# Phase 2 — Dashboard

Goals:

- Daily Sales
- Weekly Sales
- Monthly Sales
- Popular Menu Items
- Average Bill Value
- Total Orders
- Active Tables

Success Criteria:

Managers can monitor restaurant performance from a single dashboard.

---

# Phase 3 — Persistence

Goals:

- SwiftData Integration
- Persistent Restaurant Data
- Automatic Data Recovery
- Improved Startup Experience

Success Criteria:

Restaurant data survives application restarts.

---

# Phase 4 — Billing Enhancements

Goals:

- Receipt Printing
- Split Billing
- Discounts
- Tax Improvements
- Service Charges

Success Criteria:

Flexible billing workflow for different restaurant types.

---

# Phase 5 — Restaurant Management

Goals:

- Menu Management
- Categories
- Inventory
- Stock Tracking
- Reservations

Success Criteria:

Restaurant managers can maintain daily operations directly from DineFlow.

---

# Phase 6 — Reporting

Goals:

- Sales Reports
- Order Reports
- Kitchen Reports
- Staff Reports
- Export Options

Success Criteria:

Managers gain meaningful business insights.

---

# Phase 7 — Advanced Features

Goals:

- Staff Management
- Role-Based Permissions
- Customer Profiles
- Loyalty Program
- Multi-Branch Support

Success Criteria:

Support medium and large restaurant businesses.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Future possibilities:

- Cloud Synchronization
- Multi-Device Sync
- Online Ordering
- Delivery Integration
- AI Insights
- Business Forecasting

These features are not currently planned and should not influence present architecture.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every feature should improve at least one of the following:

- Simplicity
- Speed
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Offline Capability
- User Experience

Features that increase complexity without delivering significant value should be reconsidered.
